Test Format

Each attempt draws a fresh random set of 10 questions from a 50-item pool covering this unit’s objectives. Answer choices are reshuffled every attempt. Click an answer to select it, mark whether you were confident or guessing, then click Grade My Answers. Keep resetting until you score a perfect 10 out of 10 in a single attempt.

In Plain Terms

Confident-and-wrong is the category to watch for: you believe something false and have no reason to double-check it. That category is invisible on an ordinary score report, and it is what fails people on the first attempt. The Confident / Guessed buttons under each question make it visible.
