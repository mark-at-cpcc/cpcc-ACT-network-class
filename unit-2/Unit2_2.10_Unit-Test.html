Test Format

Each attempt draws 20 items from a 100-item pool covering this unit’s objectives, weighted to mirror how they are represented on the N10-009 exam. Answer choices are reshuffled every attempt. Untimed.

Test Taking Skills

Read each question and each answer. Select the best answer. Then read the question again, and each answer. If you agree with your original answer, move on; otherwise work through the answers and see which ones are easily dismissable. Review every missed question before you close this page.

Submit Your Proof of Completion (100 points)

When you finish, take a screenshot showing your final score and upload it to the submission link in Brightspace by the due date. A screenshot is your only proof — nothing here is recorded automatically. The completion standard is all 20 items answered and the missed ones reviewed, not a specific score.

CompTIA Network+ Passes at 720

The exam is scored on a 100–900 scale and passes at 720. Because the scale starts at 100 rather than zero, 720 works out to about 78% of raw points. That is a high bar: a student who treats 70% on a practice set as “good enough” fails the real exam. This course’s mastery gate of 800 exists so that your exam day has a cushion.
