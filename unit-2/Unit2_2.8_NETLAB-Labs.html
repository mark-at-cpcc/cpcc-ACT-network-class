How NETLAB Works

NETLAB+ gives you temporary access to real equipment through your browser. You book a slot, do the lab, and the system resets the pod when your time is up. You cannot permanently break anything, which is the whole point.

Getting In

  1. Always launch from Brightspace. Open the NETLAB — Student Labs folder under Coursework and click the link for the lab you are starting.
  2. Make a reservation. Choose a date and time window.
  3. Read the worksheet before your window opens. Worksheets are in the NETLAB — Lab Worksheets folder and inside NETLAB itself.
  4. Complete the lab in one continuous session, capturing screenshots as you go.
  5. Submit your screenshots to the matching lab quiz in Brightspace — each lab’s screenshots go to that lab’s own quiz.
One Session, No Save Point

If your reservation expires mid-lab, the environment resets and you begin again from step one. Book generously — if a lab is estimated at 1.25 hours, reserve closer to 2.

Reserve Early

The college has a finite number of lab pods. When the whole class books the same Sunday night, the slots are gone, and the deadline does not move because a reservation was unavailable. Book all of this unit’s windows at the start of the week.

Before You Start

Lab 02 (IPv4 Addressing) and Lab 03 (IPv6 Addressing) configure and verify addresses on the pod’s hosts and routers — do them in that order, after the Chapter 7 reading. Lab 08 (Ethernet Switching Technologies) is here because Chapter 5 introduces switches and bridges; it previews Unit 3. Three reservations this week; book all three on Tuesday.
