Test Format

Each attempt draws 10 questions at random from a 50-question pool covering the 20 most common ports and protocols on the N10-009 exam. Every question names a protocol and offers five port numbers; one is correct. Answer order reshuffles every attempt. Keep resetting until you score 10 out of 10.

The 20 Ports on This Test
