Test Format

Each attempt draws 20 questions from the 100-acronym pool, with five answer choices each. Answer choices are reshuffled every attempt. Ungraded, unlimited attempts, available all term.

In Plain Terms

Drill this whenever you have ten spare minutes. Acronyms show up in every scenario question on the exam; the faster you decode them, the more time you have for the question itself.
