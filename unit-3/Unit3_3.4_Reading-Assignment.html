How to Read a Certification Text

Read the Exam Essentials first, then the chapter, then the Exam Essentials again. Do the Written Lab as you go. Answer the Review Questions before you look at the key. The reading is where the exam is won; the videos are where it is confirmed.

Terminology Note
CompTIA’s First Hop Redundancy Protocol (FHRP) appears in the book as “High Available Routes” (Chapter 10, p. 362). Port aggregation is link aggregation on the exam (book: “Port Bonding,” p. 417).
Beyond the Exam
EIGRP internals, IS-IS, RIPng, EIGRPv6, and OSPFv3 (Chapter 10, pp. 353–371) and VTP (Chapter 11, pp. 409–411) exceed N10-009. Read once; do not drill. Appendix C (Subnetting Class A) is optional extra practice.

Reading Notes

One page per chapter as before. For Chapter 8, replace the page with a worksheet: work every subnetting example in the chapter on paper before reading the book’s answer, and keep the sheet — it is your study guide for the unit test.
