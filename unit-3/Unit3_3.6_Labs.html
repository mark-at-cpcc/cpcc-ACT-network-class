Lab 3.6.1 — Subnet Sprint

Estimated Time: 30 minutes · Classroom · Objective 1.7

Three timed rounds of paper subnetting with the magic-number method; errors go on the board and the class finds the step that broke.

Steps

  1. Round one, eight minutes: network, broadcast, and usable range for ten hosts.
  2. Round two, eight minutes: “which of these hosts are on the same subnet?” pairs.
  3. Round three, ten minutes: a four-subnet VLSM plan.
  4. Debrief: every wrong answer is traced to the step — mask, block, boundary, or off-by-one.

What to Record

Which step you broke most. That is the one to drill.

2

Lab 3.6.2 — Two Routers, One Table

Estimated Time: 45 minutes · Classroom · Objective 2.1

Build two routers and three networks in Packet Tracer or on the lab routers, route them statically, then break a route and read the table.

Steps

  1. Address three /24 networks and the link between the routers as a /30.
  2. Add static routes on each router to the networks it cannot see; ping end to end.
  3. Run show ip route and identify the C, L, and S routes.
  4. Delete one static route and predict which pings fail before you test them.
  5. Add a default route instead and explain why it restores reachability.

What to Record

The routing table before and after, annotated with which line carried each ping.

3

Lab 3.6.3 — VLAN It

Estimated Time: 45 minutes · Classroom · Objective 2.2

Two PCs on different VLANs on one switch; prove they cannot talk, then add a router-on-a-stick and prove they can.

Steps

  1. Create VLANs 10 and 20; assign one PC to each access port.
  2. Ping across — it fails. Say why in one sentence.
  3. Configure the uplink as an 802.1Q trunk and the router with two subinterfaces, one per VLAN.
  4. Set each PC’s gateway to its subinterface and ping again.
  5. Check show interfaces trunk for the native and allowed VLANs.

What to Record

The trunk output and the one line that would break it if changed.

H

Lab 3.6.4 — Take-Home: Subnet a Small Business

Estimated Time: 1 – 1.5 hours · At home · Objective 1.7

Pinehurst Dental is moving into a two-floor office and has been given 10.50.0.0/24. Plan the addressing.

  • Clinical workstations: 58 devices
  • Front office and billing: 25 devices
  • Imaging and servers: 12 devices
  • Guest Wi-Fi: 60 devices
  • Two router-to-router links

Steps

  1. Sort the needs largest first and choose each prefix, leaving room for the network and broadcast addresses.
  2. Allocate each subnet from 10.50.0.0 upward with no overlap.
  3. Fill a table: name, prefix, mask, network, first usable, last usable, broadcast, gateway (first usable).
  4. Draw a one-page diagram with each VLAN and its subnet.
  5. Check your work with any subnet calculator — after you finish, not before.

What to Record

Submit nothing; bring the table to class Thursday. Unit 3’s third ticket uses a plan like yours.
