Test Format

Each attempt draws a fresh random set of 10 questions from a 50-item pool covering this unit’s objectives. Answer choices are reshuffled every attempt. Click an answer to select it, mark whether you guessed, were semi-confident, or were confident, then click Grade My Answers. Keep resetting until you score a perfect 10 out of 10 in a single attempt.

In Plain Terms

Confident-and-wrong is the category to watch for: you believe something false and have no reason to double-check it. That category is invisible on an ordinary score report, and it is what fails people on the first attempt. The Guessed / Semi-Confident / Confident buttons under each question make it visible — and so is the quieter trap, the guess that happened to be right.
