Ninety questions in the exam’s domain proportions, timed at 90 minutes, scored on the course’s 100–900 model. Ungraded and expected. Retake it as often as you like; each attempt draws a fresh set.

Exam Format

21 Networking Concepts, 18 Network Implementation, 17 Network Operations, 12 Network Security, 22 Network Troubleshooting, drawn from the unit-test pools of all six units. Results show a scaled score, pass or below, the 800 gate, and your score by domain.

Scoring Is a Model

CompTIA does not publish how it scales scores. This exam uses the course model, 100 + 800 × fraction correct, so 720 corresponds to about 78% correct and 800 to 87.5%.
