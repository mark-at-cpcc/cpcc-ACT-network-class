Subnet Warm-Ups (Design Idea P1)

Every class meeting opens with one five-minute subnetting problem, worked on paper before the ticket. We check answers together in class. The method is on the 3.A Subnetting Reference Card.

Lab 6.6.1 — Troubleshooting Stations

Estimated Time: 60 minutes · Classroom · Objectives 5.1–5.5

Five stations in Packet Tracer or on lab gear: bad cable, wrong mask, duplicate IP, wrong VLAN, DNS pointed at nothing. Solve each with the seven steps and write the ticket before moving on.

2

Lab 6.6.2 — Diagnose a Real One

Estimated Time: 45 minutes · Classroom · Objectives 5.1, 5.5

A genuine fault from an authorized network or a documented case: three ranked theories in writing, narrow by halves, change one thing at a time, write the ticket.

3

Lab 6.6.3 — Check the AI, Network+ Edition

Estimated Time: 30 minutes · Classroom · Objective 5.5

Ask an AI tool five objective-based questions, including one subnetting problem. Verify every answer against the objectives, the book, or your own subnet math; then challenge one correct answer and see whether it holds its ground.

T

Ticket of the Day

One ticket per class meeting, worked on your own

This unit’s three tickets live in Section 6.11 Ticket Exercises: CRCs on the Third Floor (Tuesday), Fine Until the Afternoon (Wednesday), and The Branch That Can’t Reach Anything (Thursday). Each takes about twenty minutes.
