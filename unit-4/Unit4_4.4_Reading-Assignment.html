Sybex CompTIA Network+ Study Guide: Exam N10-009, 6th ed. (Lammle & Buhagiar), ISBN 978-1-394-23560-5. 144 pages this unit — about 10.5 hrs. Reading notes are expected and ungraded.

How to Read a Certification Text

Read the Exam Essentials first, then the chapter, then the Exam Essentials again. Do the Written Lab as you go. Answer the Review Questions before you look at the key. The reading is where the exam is won; the videos are where it is confirmed.

Terminology Note
The book treats WEP, TKIP, and WPA in detail (Chapter 12, pp. 475–479); N10-009 expects you to know they are obsolete and that WPA2 and WPA3 are the current standards.
Beyond the Exam
Chapter 12’s cellular-technology history (pp. 436–438) and Chapter 13’s VNC detail exceed the objectives. Read once.
