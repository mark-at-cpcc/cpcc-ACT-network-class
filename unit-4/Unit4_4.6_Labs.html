Lab 4.6.1 — Walk the Floor

Estimated Time: 45 minutes · Classroom · Objective 2.3

Survey the floor with a free Wi-Fi analyzer app on your phone: channels, overlap, and signal strength at five marked points.

Steps

  1. At each point, record every SSID you see with its channel, band, and signal in dBm.
  2. Mark co-channel neighbors — two APs on the same channel both above −70 dBm.
  3. Sketch the floor with a rough heat map: green above −67, yellow to −75, red below.
  4. Recommend one channel or power change and predict its effect.
2

Lab 4.6.2 — Three Ways In

Estimated Time: 40 minutes · Classroom · Objective 3.5

Connect to the same lab switch by console, SSH, and web GUI, and compare what each can and cannot do.

Steps

  1. Console with the rollover or USB cable; note that it works with the network unplugged.
  2. SSH to the management address; run show running-config.
  3. Web GUI over HTTPS; find the same setting you viewed by CLI.
  4. Fill a table: needs network? encrypted? scriptable? works when production is down?
3

Lab 4.6.3 — Read the Logs (on your own this unit)

Estimated Time: 40 minutes · Independent · Objectives 3.2, 3.1

Given a syslog excerpt and an SNMP trap list (below), find the outage, its start time, and its cause, and draft the incident note.

<189>14:02:11 SW-CORE %LINK-5-CHANGED: Interface Gi1/0/48, changed state to down
<187>14:02:11 SW-CORE %LINEPROTO-3-UPDOWN: Line protocol on Gi1/0/48 down
<190>13:58:40 SW-CORE %SYS-6-CONFIG_I: Configured from console by jdoe on vty0
TRAP 14:02:12 linkDown ifIndex=48 SW-CORE
TRAP 14:09:30 linkUp   ifIndex=48 SW-CORE

What to Record

The severity of each line by number and name, the sequence of events, and one sentence on what change management would have required before 13:58.

H

Lab 4.6.4 — Take-Home: Survey Your Own Wi-Fi

Estimated Time: 1 hour · At home · Objective 2.3

Survey your own home Wi-Fi at four spots, make one change, and measure it.

Your Own Equipment Only

Run this lab only on the network you own or are explicitly authorized to use. Never run scanning, capture, or configuration changes against anything else.

Steps

  1. Record band, channel, and signal (dBm) at four spots, and your neighbors’ networks on the same channel.
  2. Check your router’s security: WPA2 or WPA3? WPS on or off? Default admin password changed?
  3. Make one change on your own router (channel, band steering, or AP placement) and re-measure the four spots.
  4. Write a one-page memo: before, change, after, and what you would do next.

Ticket of the Day

One ticket per class meeting, worked on your own

This unit’s three tickets live in Section 4.11 Ticket Exercises: The Conference Room Dead Zone (Tuesday), Everyone Is Slow on the VPN (Wednesday), and Who Changed the Core Switch? (on your own) (Thursday). Each takes about twenty minutes.

What These Labs Give You

Wireless questions on the exam are site surveys in disguise, and operations questions are log excerpts in disguise. Reading both, by hand, is the preparation.
