A Viewing Plan

Sixteen videos, just under three hours — the heaviest video week until Unit 5, so spread it over four evenings. Tuesday (with Chapter 5): Networking Devices, Networking Functions, DHCP, Configuring DHCP — 47 minutes. Wednesday (with Chapter 6): Introduction to IP, Common Ports, Other Useful Protocols, Network Communication — 47 minutes. Thursday (with Chapter 7): Binary Math, IPv4 Addressing, Classful Subnetting, IPv6 Addressing, IPv6 and SLAAC — 50 minutes. Friday or the weekend: An Overview of DNS, DNS Records, Time Protocols — 31 minutes. Watch each after its reading; Common Ports is worth a second viewing before the unit test.

Video Notes

One line per video, as before. For Common Ports, do not take notes — pause the video at each port and say the number before he does. For Binary Math, work his examples on paper; it is the one video this unit where watching is not enough.
