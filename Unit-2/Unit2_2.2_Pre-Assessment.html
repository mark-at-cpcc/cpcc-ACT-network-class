This is a diagnostic, not a test, and it is ungraded. Complete it on your own, before the reading and the videos. It is expected by the end of the unit’s first day (Tuesday) and stays open all week — see the Course Schedule.

No Preparation Needed

Do not study for this and do not look anything up. It measures what you bring to the unit. Some questions will feel familiar — others will not, and that is the point.

Read This First

You are expected to get questions wrong; you have not been taught this material yet. Attempting to retrieve an answer you do not have makes the correct answer stick better when you meet it later — guessing today is doing real work. Select an answer for each question, click Grade My Answers, and read what you missed. Reset clears the set.
