Twenty-five videos, about three and a quarter hours — the heaviest video week. Four evenings: Tuesday (Ch 16): Disaster Recovery, Network Redundancy, Installing Networks, Power, Environmental Factors. Wednesday (Ch 17): Designing the Cloud, Cloud Models, Software Defined Networking, Virtual Extensible LAN, Zero Trust, Infrastructure as Code. Weekend (Ch 21): Denial of Service, VLAN Hopping, MAC Flooding, ARP and DNS Poisoning, Rogue Services, Social Engineering, Malware, Device Security, Security Rules.
