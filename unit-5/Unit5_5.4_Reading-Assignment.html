How to Read a Certification Text

Read the Exam Essentials first, then the chapter, then the Exam Essentials again. Do the Written Lab as you go. Answer the Review Questions before you look at the key. The reading is where the exam is won; the videos are where it is confirmed.

Terminology Note
Secure Access Service Edge (SASE) appears in the book as “Secure Access Secure Edge” (Chapter 17, p. 634). Book typo: the Chapter 21 contents entry “Device Gardening” (p. 832) is device hardening.
Beyond the Exam
Chapter 20’s detailed treatment of specific encryption algorithms goes past N10-009’s “explain the importance” level. Know what each protects; leave the internals to Security+.

Reading Notes

Make the attack table in 5.3 your own: one line per attack with the symptom you would see and the control that stops it. Unit 6’s tickets assume it.
