5.A — Practice Exam
Return to Index

Your first full-length practice exam: 90 questions drawn by the exam’s domain weights from the practice sets of all six units. Ungraded and expected. Take it once this unit; it opens with the unit’s other assessments — see the Course Schedule.

Exam Format

90 questions in the N10-009 domain proportions — 21 Networking Concepts, 18 Network Implementation, 17 Network Operations, 12 Network Security, 22 Network Troubleshooting — drawn fresh every attempt. Timed at 90 minutes. Results show a scaled score on the 100–900 model and a per-domain breakdown.

How to Read Your Score

The exam passes at 720; the course’s mastery gate is 800. This score is guidance, not a gate — Domain 5 (troubleshooting) is taught in Unit 6, so expect those questions to feel new. Use the domain breakdown to decide where your next study hours go.
