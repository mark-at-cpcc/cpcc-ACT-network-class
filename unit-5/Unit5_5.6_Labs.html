Lab 5.6.1 — DR Tabletop

Estimated Time: 40 minutes · Classroom · Objective 3.3

The MDF floods overnight. Walk the disaster recovery plan as a team: who is called, which services come back first, what the RTO and RPO allow, and whether a hot, warm, or cold site meets them.

2

Lab 5.6.2 — Rule Order

Estimated Time: 30 minutes · Classroom · Objective 4.3

Order a set of ACL rules so the stated policy works, then find the one rule that silently breaks everything below it. Remember: top-down, first match wins, implicit deny at the end.

3

Lab 5.6.3 — Name That Attack

Estimated Time: 30 minutes · Classroom · Objectives 4.2, 4.3

Symptom cards (“MAC table full,” “two replies for the gateway,” “unknown SSID matching ours”) sorted to the attack and the control that stops it, then defended to the room.

H

Lab 5.6.4 — Take-Home: Segment Your Home

Estimated Time: 1 – 1.5 hours · At home · Objectives 4.1, 4.3

Audit your own router the way you would a small business’s.

Your Own Equipment Only

Run this lab only on the network you own or are explicitly authorized to use. Never run scanning, capture, or configuration changes against anything else.

Checklist

  1. Credentials: is the admin password the default? Change it.
  2. Firmware: what version, and is an update available?
  3. Remote management: is admin access open from the internet? It should be off.
  4. Segmentation: is there a guest network, and are smart-home devices on it or on your main network?
  5. Services: is WPS on? UPnP? Note each and why you would disable it.

What to Record

A one-page memo: what you found, what you changed, and what you left and why.

T

Ticket of the Day

One ticket per class meeting, worked on your own

This unit’s three tickets live in Section 5.11 Ticket Exercises: Two Gateways Answered (Tuesday), The Site Is Down, the Server Is Fine (Wednesday), and Can We Survive Losing the Building? (Thursday). Each takes about twenty minutes.

What These Labs Give You

Domain 4 questions describe a symptom and ask for the attack, or describe an attack and ask for the control. The labs drill both directions.
